### Image: metacraft:beside/brewing/chapter_drinks The chapters' own drinks, brewed the same way. Each one sends you fast or slow (Släggan: slow and strong, or fast and weak), harder the drunker you are, and past a point it blacks you out: you come to somewhere else with a headache, a little hurt and blind for a moment.

### Header: Alcoholic Beverage
- 4 × <citem 'minecraft:potato'>
- 2 × <citem 'minecraft:sugar'>

Cook for 10 min. No barrel.

### Header: Nyckeln Patch Drink
- 2 × <citem 'minecraft:apple'>
- 1 × <citem 'minecraft:sugar'>

Cook for 2 min. No barrel. <gray>No alcohol.</gray>

### Header: Släggan Patch Drink
- 4 × <citem 'minecraft:potato'>
- 1 × <citem 'minecraft:suspicious_stew'>

Cook for 8 min, then distil once. No barrel.

### Header: Spiken Patch Drink

- 4 × <citem 'minecraft:sweet_berries'>
