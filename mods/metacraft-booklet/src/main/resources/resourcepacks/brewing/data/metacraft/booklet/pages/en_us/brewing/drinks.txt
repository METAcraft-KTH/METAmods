### Image: metacraft:beside/brewing/drinks Brewery's own drinks. Cook and age close to the times given for the best quality; far off and the drink comes out poor, or not at all.

### Header: Apple Liquor
- 12 × <citem 'minecraft:apple'>

Cook for 16 min, then distil once, then age in acacia barrel for 3 h 20 min.
