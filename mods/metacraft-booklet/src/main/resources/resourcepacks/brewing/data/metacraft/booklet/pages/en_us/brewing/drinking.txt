Every alcoholic drink adds to how drunk you are, more for strong drinks and more again for poor ones. It wears off with time.

- A little drunk, you get weak and slow.
- More, and you stagger and the world spins.
- Much more, and it gets dark and the alcohol starts to hurt. Stop there.
