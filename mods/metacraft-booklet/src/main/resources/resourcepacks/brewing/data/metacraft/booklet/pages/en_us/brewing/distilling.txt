### Image: metacraft:beside/brewing/distilling Vodka, rum, apple liquor and the chapter spirits come out of the cauldron as a mixture that needs distilling.

- Fuel a <citem 'minecraft:brewing_stand'> with <citem 'minecraft:blaze_powder'>.
- Put the mixtures in the bottle slots and leave the ingredient slot <bold>empty</bold>.
- Wait for one run. A recipe that says "distil twice" wants a second.
