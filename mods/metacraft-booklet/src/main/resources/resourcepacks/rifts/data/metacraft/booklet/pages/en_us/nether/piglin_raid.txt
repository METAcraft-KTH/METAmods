### Header: Sizes
A rift's size is the number of blocks inside its frame. A plain portal is 6 blocks, so it is small.
- <bold>Small</bold>, up to 20 blocks: 3 waves.
- <bold>Medium</bold>, 21 to 60 blocks: 5 waves.
- <bold>Large</bold>, 61 blocks or more: 7 waves. Chained ghasts come in waves 5 and 7, and the Piglin General in the last.

### Header: The raid
Everyone within 48 blocks takes part. Each player after the first brings a quarter more piglins and hoglins, up to twice as many. A boss bar counts down the wave; the next one comes when the last mob is dead. After 90 seconds, the stragglers glow.<nl2>Rift mobs that stray more than 64 blocks are pulled back to the rift. Gold won't help you: rift piglins attack players in gold armour and never barter.<nl2>If nobody stays within 48 blocks for 3 minutes, the rift collapses with no reward. Breaking a frame block collapses a raiding rift too.

### Header: Who comes through
Crossbow piglins, spear piglins and hoglins make up most of a wave. Later waves add ghasts with a piglin on board, striders with riders, and brutes with golden axes. From wave 2, a <pagelink 'metacraft:nether/mobs/drummer'><u>Piglin Drummer</u></pagelink> leads each wave. See <pagelink 'metacraft:nether/mobs'><u>New Mobs</u></pagelink> for the rest.

### Header: The Piglin General
### Image: metacraft:beside/rifts/general A piglin brute half again as big as one, with a red banner on its head and a golden axe. It comes in the last wave of a large rift, with 300 health, six times a brute's, and armour nearly a diamond set's. It has a red boss bar of its own.

### Body:
Killed by a player, it drops:
- its head, a <citem 'minecraft:piglin_head'> named Piglin General;
- a <citem 'minecraft:blaze_rod'>;
- 4 to 8 <citem 'minecraft:quartz'>;
- one time in four, a <citem 'minecraft:piglin_banner_pattern'>.

### Header: Spoils
Rift piglins only drop loot when a player kills them, and no rift mob drops its gear.
- Piglins: up to 2 <citem 'minecraft:quartz'> and 3 gold nuggets, often <citem 'minecraft:glowstone_dust'>, sometimes <citem 'minecraft:nether_wart'>.
- Brutes: the same, and always a <citem 'minecraft:blaze_rod'>.
- Piglin Drummers: the same, and always a <citem 'minecraft:gold_ingot'>.
- Hoglins, ghasts and striders: their usual drops.

### Header: Winning
Win the last wave and the rift bursts into gold ingots and glowstone dust:
- <bold>Small:</bold> 3 to 5 gold, 4 to 8 glowstone.
- <bold>Medium:</bold> 6 to 10 gold, 8 to 16 glowstone.
- <bold>Large:</bold> 12 to 18 gold, 16 to 24 glowstone.

### Header: The frame
When a torn rift closes, won or lost, its obsidian frame turns to <citem 'minecraft:crying_obsidian'>. Keep it: see <pagelink 'metacraft:rifts/stabilising'><u>Stabilising</u></pagelink>.
