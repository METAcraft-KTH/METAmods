When the nether opens, it will not be the nether you know.

- It is <bold>amplified</bold>: the ground climbs and drops far more steeply than before.
- It has <bold>more biomes</bold> to find.
- Its <bold>roof is closed</bold>: there is no getting up above the bedrock ceiling.<nl>
