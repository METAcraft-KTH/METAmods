### Header: Three lights
- <bold>First light:</bold> light a nether portal frame in the overworld and a rift opens. Everyone within 32 blocks is warned in chat.
- <bold>Second light:</bold> use <citem 'minecraft:flint_and_steel'> or a <citem 'minecraft:fire_charge'> on the rift or its frame. It strains and its cracks spread, one light from tearing. Leave it for 5 minutes and it settles back.
- <bold>Third light:</bold> it tears open, reddening, with the nether showing through. A horn sounds, and the first wave comes 10 seconds later.<nl>

Break a frame block and a quiet rift fades.<nl>

<gray>The realm can only take 4 torn rifts at once. A third light is refused until one closes.</gray><nl>

What comes through after the third light: see <pagelink 'metacraft:nether/piglin_raid'><u>Piglin Raid</u></pagelink>.
