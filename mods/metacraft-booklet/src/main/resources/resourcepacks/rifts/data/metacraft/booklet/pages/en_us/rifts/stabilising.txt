### Header: The realm will stabilise in time
While the realm is unstable, an <citem 'minecraft:ender_eye'> can't be crafted. When it steadies, waiting rifts turn into working nether portals, new portals light as normal, and raids still running finish as usual.

### Header: Crying obsidian
After that, a frame of <citem 'minecraft:crying_obsidian'> in the overworld opens a rift on purpose. Light it with flint and steel or a fire charge, and the three lights, sizes and spoils work as before. Any closed shape works, up to 21 blocks wide and tall.<nl2>Each crying-obsidian frame gives one raid, then crumbles away.
