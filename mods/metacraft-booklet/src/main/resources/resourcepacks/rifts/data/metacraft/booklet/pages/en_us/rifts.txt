### Header: The realm is unstable
For now a lit nether portal takes you nowhere. It opens a <bold>rift</bold>: the portal flickers but will not hold, and fine cracks run across it. Light it again and they spread; tear it open and the nether shows through the holes.<nl>

Light a rift twice more and it tears open. Piglins come through in waves, led by drummers and bombed from chained ghasts: a <pagelink 'metacraft:nether/piglin_raid'><u>Piglin Raid</u></pagelink>. Beat every wave and the rift pays out in gold and glowstone.
