### Header: Tells
### Image: metacraft:beside/rifts/grenadier A piglin in goggles, with a golden sword. It rides the deck of a chained ghast and drops its bombs. When the ghast falls, it fights on foot.

### Header: Magma bombs
- A ring of flame on the ground marks where a bomb will land, a second before it does. Step out of it.
- A bomb hurts and knocks you back, but never breaks blocks and never hurts rift mobs.
- It leaves a burning patch for 2 seconds. Stand in it and you catch fire.

### Header: On foot
Every 4 seconds it stops, turns to a player it can see 4 to 12 blocks away, and lobs a bomb at their feet. Closer than that, it uses its sword. It has 20 health.

### Header: Weaknesses
The throw is slow, and the ring shows you where it lands: keep moving, or close in and fight it up close.
