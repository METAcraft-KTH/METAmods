### Header: Music on the go
Right-click the Boombox to open it, put a music disc in the middle slot and press Play. It keeps playing while you carry it.

Better boomboxes add more: rows of disc storage (right-click a stored disc to play it), Playlist to keep the music going from disc to disc, Shuffle to pick the next disc at random, Skip, and Volume and Speed controls. A louder boombox carries further, up to 64 blocks.

### Header: Placing it
Place it and it plays on as a small block. A redstone signal starts it, and a comparator reads the song. Break it and it drops with its discs inside, like a shulker box. Hoppers can fill and empty its storage.

### Header: Getting one
It can't be crafted. A <bold>Piglin Drummer</bold>, from the nether and its rifts, drops one now and then, more often with Looting, sometimes with a disc already in it.
