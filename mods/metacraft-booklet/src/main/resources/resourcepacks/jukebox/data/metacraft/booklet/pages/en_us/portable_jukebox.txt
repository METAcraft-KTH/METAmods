### Header: Music on the go
Right-click the Portable Jukebox to open it: one slot for a music disc, and Play and Stop. It keeps playing while you carry it.

Place it and it stays a small block that plays on. A redstone signal starts it, and a comparator reads it.

### Header: Getting one
It can't be crafted. A <bold>Piglin Drummer</bold>, from the nether and its rifts, drops one now and then, more often with Looting, sometimes with a disc already in it.
