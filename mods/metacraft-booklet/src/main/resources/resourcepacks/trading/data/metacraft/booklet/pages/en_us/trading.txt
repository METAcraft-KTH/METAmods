### Header: Setting up
Craft a <citem 'metacraft:shop'> from 3 wool across the top, a barrel between two planks in the middle, and an iron ingot between two planks at the bottom. Place it on top of a <citem 'minecraft:barrel'> or on its side: one shop to a barrel. Fill the barrel with what you sell.

### Header: Your trades
Use your shop to set its trades (sneak to see it as a buyer does): up to 9, each a row of three slots. <bold>1</bold> and <bold>2</bold> are the price (the second is optional), <bold>R</bold> is what the buyer gets.
- Click a slot with an item to put it there: left-click for the whole stack, right-click for one.
- With an empty hand, left-click adds one and right-click takes one away. Hold shift to add or take a stack.<nl>

The trades are saved when you close the menu.

### Header: Buying
Anyone else who uses the shop, or its barrel, gets a trading menu like a villager's. A trade works while the barrel holds what it sells and has room for the payment, which goes into the barrel. Only you can open the barrel itself.
- A hopper under the barrel takes the payments instead.
- Hoppers pointing into the barrel add their items to the stock.
- No hopper can put items into the barrel or take them out.

### Header: Shop keys
Use your shop with a <citem 'minecraft:gold_ingot'> in hand and the ingot becomes a key to it. Whoever holds the key, in either hand, can change the trades and open the barrel as you can.

### Header: Moving a shop
Break the shop and it keeps its trades: place it on another barrel and they are back. To wipe them, put it through a grindstone.
