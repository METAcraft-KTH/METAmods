### Header: In the caves
A <bold>Pirkko</bold> is a small mob that lives in dark caves below sea level, in the Overworld, in groups of one to three. Not in the deep dark or under mushroom fields.

It has 2 hearts. It chases players within 24 blocks and bites for half a heart, however hard the difficulty. On Peaceful it still turns up, but only wanders.

A Pirkko that nobody has named, leashed or seated wanders off after ten minutes and leaves nothing.

### Header: Colours
Every Pirkko has one of 18 colours: the 16 dyes, <bold>Cerise</bold> and <bold>Laserviolet</bold>. The colour follows how warm the land above the cave is: frozen places mostly give Cerise and blues, deserts and badlands mostly Laserviolet, red and orange. One in ten is any colour at all.

### Header: Heads
Kill one and it drops exactly one <citem 'pirkko:pirkko_cerise'> head in its colour; Looting gives no more. Place it on a floor or a wall like a mob head, or in an item frame to show the whole Pirkko. Collect them all.
