### Image: metacraft:beside/decorating/mailbox A mailbox belongs to whoever places it. Other players leave things in it; only the owner takes them out.<nl2>The little flag goes up when there is mail.

### Header: Making one
### Image: metacraft:beside/recipe/mailbox Makes two.<nl2>Any wood works: the log and the slabs decide which mailbox you get.

### Header: Sending
Right-click someone else's mailbox to open your own compartment in it: six slots that only you and the owner see. Put things in and close it. Until the owner takes them, you can take them back out.

### Header: Receiving
Right-click your own mailbox to see a head for everyone who has left you something; hover over a head to see what. Click it to open that compartment and take the things out.

### Body:
Breaking a mailbox drops everything in it. The <pagelink 'metacraft:decorating/tools'><u>hammer</u></pagelink> turns it to face another way.
