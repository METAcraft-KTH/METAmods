### Header: Hammer
### Image: metacraft:beside/recipe/hammer The <citem 'polydecorations:hammer'> turns blocks round without breaking them.

Right-click or hit a placed block with it to turn it, without breaking it:
- logs and other pillars: which way they lie;
- slabs: top half or bottom half;
- stairs: click the flat step to turn them, the full-width half to flip them upside down, the narrow half to change their corner shape;
- trapdoors, fence gates and mailboxes: which way they face;
- bamboo: its leaves;
- sign posts: a sign 15° at a time.

### Header: Trowel
### Image: metacraft:beside/recipe/trowel The <citem 'polydecorations:trowel'> lays blocks for you, at random.

### Image: metacraft:beside/decorating/trowel Right-click with it and it places a random block from your hotbar.<nl2>Put cobblestone, mossy cobblestone and gravel there and a path comes out looking worn in.

### Body:
Keep your off hand free of blocks: with a block there, the trowel does nothing.

Both tools only work where you are allowed to build.
