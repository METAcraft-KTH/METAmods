### Image: metacraft:beside/decorating/sign_post A sign post is an arrow-shaped sign on a fence or wall: point the way to your base, the shop, the portal.<nl2>Each post takes two, one above the other.

### Header: Making them
### Image: metacraft:beside/recipe/sign_post Makes two.<nl2>Any wood works: the planks decide which.

### Header: Putting one up
Right-click the upper or lower half of a fence or wall with a sign post. The sign turns to face you, in steps of 15°, and the sign editor opens: one line of text.

### Header: Changing it
Stand in front of the sign and:
- right-click it to edit the text;
- use a dye, a <citem 'minecraft:glow_ink_sac'> or <citem 'minecraft:honeycomb'> as on any sign;
- <bold>sneak</bold> and right-click with an empty hand to point the arrow the other way;
- right-click with an axe to take the sign back off.

### Body:
The <pagelink 'metacraft:decorating/tools'><u>hammer</u></pagelink> turns a sign 15° at a time.
