### Header: Lanterns on walls
### Image: metacraft:beside/decorating/lantern <bold>Sneak</bold> and right-click the side of a block with a lantern to hang it on the wall, on a bracket. Fences and walls take them too.<nl2>Every lantern works: plain, soul and copper.

### Body:
Where a lantern would have nothing to stand on or hang from, clicking the side of a block puts it on the wall even without sneaking.

### Header: Leads between fences
### Image: metacraft:beside/decorating/lead Right-click a fence with a <citem 'minecraft:lead'> to tie one end to it, then right-click another fence to tie the other end.<nl2>No animal needed: a washing line, a railing, a line for bunting.

### Body:
Hit either knot to take the lead down again.
