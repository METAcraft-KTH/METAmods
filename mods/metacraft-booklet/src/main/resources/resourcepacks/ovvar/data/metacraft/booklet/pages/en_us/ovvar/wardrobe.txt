### Header: It follows you
What you have sewn on, and the patches you own but have not sewn — your <bold>stash</bold> — are stored once for all the servers. Sew on one server, log into another wearing the same ovve; lose the item and a new ovve of that chapter shows your design again. Pockets and enchantments stay with the item; the look and the patches travel.

### Header: /ovvar stash
### Image: metacraft:beside/ovvar/stash <yellow>/ovvar stash</yellow> opens it.<nl2><bold>Top:</bold> an ovve per chapter you own.<nl><bold>Left:</bold> your stash.<nl><bold>Right:</bold> your ovve now; the arrows turn it.<nl><bold>Bottom:</bold> what you can do here.

Anything this server does not allow is greyed out, with the reason when you hover it.

- <bold>Left-click</bold> a patch to take one out as an item: sew it on any stand wearing your ovve, or trade it.
- The <bold>chest</bold> button puts every patch item you carry back into the stash.
- Where private sewing sessions are on, <bold>right-click</bold> a patch instead: a stand wearing your ovve appears in front of you, your hotbar gets the patch and a pair of shears, and you sew straight from the stash. Walk away, or run <yellow>/ovvar stash done</yellow>, and it is over.

### Header: Minigame servers
On a minigame server the wardrobe is look-only: nothing is sewn there, and any patch that lands in your inventory is banked into your stash at once, so nothing is lost to a wiped inventory. Take it to a survival server to sew.

### Header: Looking at others
<yellow>/ovvar look</yellow> followed by a name shows somebody's ovve as they have it, whether they are online or not. Their stash stays theirs.
