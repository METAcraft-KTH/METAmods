### Header: On a stand
### Image: metacraft:beside/ovvar/stand Put your ovve on an <citem 'minecraft:armor_stand'>.<nl2>Hold a patch and look at the stand: the patch shows <bold>washed-out</bold> where it will go, and the action bar names the spot.<nl2>Right-click to sew it on.

<bold>Sneak</bold> to aim at the far side of the part you are looking at — the back of the body, the back of an arm. The aim follows the stand's pose, so a posed stand works too.

### Header: The stitching
Where the stitching minigame is on, the right-click opens the sewing instead: the patch lies on the cloth and the seam runs around its edge. The needle sits on the next hole — click it to pull the thread through. The last pull sews the patch on. Escape, or "Cut the thread", gives up; the patch only leaves your hand when the seam is done.

### Header: Unpicking
<citem 'minecraft:shears'> on a sewn patch take it off the ovve and hand it back to you.

### Header: Taking the ovve back
Click the stand's body or an arm with an <bold>empty hand</bold> and the whole ovve, patches and all, comes off into your hand. Clicking its legs does the same.

Only the owner of an ovve can sew on it or unpick from it.
