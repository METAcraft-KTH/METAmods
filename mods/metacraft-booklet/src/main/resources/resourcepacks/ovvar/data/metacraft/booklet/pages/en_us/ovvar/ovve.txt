### Header: One garment, one item
### Image: metacraft:beside/ovvar/chapters One ovve per chapter: <citem 'ovvar:data_ovve'> in cerise, <citem 'ovvar:it_ovve'> in purple, the older <citem 'ovvar:it_kisel_ovve'>, and the <citem 'ovvar:media_frack'>.

Each ovve is a single item that goes in the <bold>legs</bold> slot: drag it in, or shift-click it. Right-clicking does not equip it (see pockets). It protects like leather and never wears out.

The <bold>Media frack</bold> is a tailcoat, so it goes in the <bold>chest</bold> slot instead. It is its own top: there is nothing to roll up or down, and patches go only on the coat. Its pockets work like an ovve's.

### Header: The top
Sneak and right-click the ovve to roll its top <bold>up</bold> or <bold>down</bold>. With the top up and nothing in your chest slot the sleeves show; a chestplate goes on over it as usual and hides the top until it comes off again. The top is not a separate item — it comes and goes with the ovve.

### Header: Pockets
The ovve is also a bundle. Click items onto it to fill it, in your inventory or straight onto its slot while you wear it; right-click and hold to empty it. It holds twice what a bundle does.

### Header: Whose ovve it is
An ovve becomes the property of the first player to pick it up. Somebody else's ovve will not go on you and nobody but you can sew on yours — so an ovve can be traded, but the design on it is its owner's. See <pagelink 'metacraft:ovvar/wardrobe'><u>Wardrobe</u></pagelink>.
