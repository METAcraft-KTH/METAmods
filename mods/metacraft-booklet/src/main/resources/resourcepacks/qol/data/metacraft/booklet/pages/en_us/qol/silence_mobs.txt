### Image: metacraft:beside/qol/echo_fork Right-click a mob with the echo fork and it goes quiet. Right-click it again to undo it.<nl2>The echo fork is never used up, and the mob keeps its name.

### Header: Making one
### Image: metacraft:beside/recipe/echo_fork Two sculk over an echo shard over an iron ingot, shaped like a tuning fork.

### Header: With a name tag
A name tag named "silence me" silences a mob too, and one named "unsilence me" undoes it. The name tag is used up, but the mob keeps its own name.
