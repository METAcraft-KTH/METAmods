### Image: metacraft:beside/qol/muffler Right-click a mob with the muffler and it goes quiet. Right-click it again to undo it.<nl2>The muffler is never used up, and the mob keeps its name.

### Header: Making one
### Image: metacraft:beside/recipe/muffler Wool of any colour, an amethyst shard and string, in any order.

### Header: With a name tag
A name tag named "silence me" silences a mob too, and one named "unsilence me" undoes it. The name tag is used up, but the mob keeps its own name.
