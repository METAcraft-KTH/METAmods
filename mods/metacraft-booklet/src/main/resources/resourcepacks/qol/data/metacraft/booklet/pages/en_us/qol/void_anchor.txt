### Image: metacraft:beside/qol/void_anchor A void anchor catches you when you fall into the End's void. Space cracks open below you, you sink into the crack, and you step out next to your anchor.<nl2>It only works in the End.

### Header: Making one
### Image: metacraft:beside/recipe/void_anchor Like a respawn anchor, with eyes of ender where the glowstone goes.

### Header: Charging it
Right-click it with an end crystal to add a charge, up to four; the lights on its sides show how many. Each rescue uses one. A dispenser with end crystals in it charges the anchor it faces.

### Header: Binding to it
Right-click it with an empty hand. You are bound to one anchor at a time, so binding another moves you over. Friends can bind to the same anchor and share its charges.

### Header: Falling
### Image: metacraft:beside/qol/rift Drop below the bottom of the End and you stop in mid-air, looking down, while space cracks open under you. You sink into the crack, everything goes white, and you step out of a crack on top of your anchor. The anchor loses a charge.<nl2>Gliding with an elytra? It folds when the crack catches you.

### Body:
If your anchor is empty, broken or blocked in, you are told so as you fall, and you fall the way you would without one.
