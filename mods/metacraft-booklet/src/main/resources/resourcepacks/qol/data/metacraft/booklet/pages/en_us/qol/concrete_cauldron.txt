### Image: metacraft:beside/qol/concrete Throw concrete powder into a cauldron with water in it and it turns into concrete. The whole stack you threw changes, for one level of water.<nl2>So throw full stacks: 64 powder costs the same water as one.

### Body:
A full cauldron does three stacks. Fill it again with a water bucket, or let rain or dripping dripstone do it. A hopper under the cauldron picks up the concrete.
