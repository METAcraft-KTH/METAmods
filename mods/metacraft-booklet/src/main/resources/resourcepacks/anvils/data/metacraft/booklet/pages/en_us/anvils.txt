### Header: Never too expensive
An anvil never says "Too Expensive!": a job that would cost 40 levels or more costs 39.

### Header: Only enchanting adds up
In vanilla every trip to the anvil makes the next one dearer. Here only adding enchantments does. Repairing and renaming never make an item cost more later.

### Header: Cheap renaming
Renaming alone costs about one level, however many times the item has been to an anvil.

### Header: Repairs
- One <citem 'minecraft:netherite_ingot'> repairs netherite gear fully, and so do four <citem 'minecraft:netherite_scrap'>.
- A bow can have both Mending and Infinity.
