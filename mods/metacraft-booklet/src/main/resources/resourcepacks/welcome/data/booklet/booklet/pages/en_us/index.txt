### Image: metacraft:logo
You play METAcraft on a vanilla client: there is nothing to install. The server sends its own resource pack when you join.<nl>

What it adds follows a few ideas:
- <bold>Student culture:</bold> the chapters' traditions, brought into the game. Their drinks, their banners, their colours, the ovve and its patches.
- <bold>Vanilla+:</bold> additions that feel like they could be vanilla, and fit in with the blocks and items you know.
- <bold>The Nether</bold> is held back at first. For now its portals open rifts, and the rifts bring piglin raids.
- <bold>The End</bold> gets the void anchor, a way back when you fall into the void.
