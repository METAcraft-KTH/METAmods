Type <bold>/gesture</bold> to open the gestures menu, and pick one. Your character plays it for everyone near you, and you watch from outside, as they see it. Some gestures play a song.<nl2>You have to stand on the ground to start one. It ends by itself, or sneak to stop it early.<nl2>The menu is also in your Quick Actions.
