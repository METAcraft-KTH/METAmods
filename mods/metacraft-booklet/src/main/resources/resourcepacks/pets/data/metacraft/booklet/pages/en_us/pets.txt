### Header: Trusted players
<bold>Sneak and right-click</bold> a pet you tamed to choose who else may look after it, from the players within 16 blocks. Trusted players can make it sit and stand, and it never attacks them.

A pet follows whoever right-clicked it last, you or a trusted player.

### Header: Safe from friends
You and your trusted players can't hurt your pet, arrows included.

### Header: It comes along
A pet that is following you, and anything on your lead, comes with you when you teleport or go through a portal, the End's exit portal too. Pets that are sitting stay where they are.

### Header: Parrots
Parrots can be bred with seeds. A baby takes one parent's colour, and belongs to its parent's owner. Seeds also heal a tame parrot.
