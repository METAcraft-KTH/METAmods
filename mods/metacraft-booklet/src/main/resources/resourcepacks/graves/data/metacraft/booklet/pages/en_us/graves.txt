### Header: Where you died
When you die, your items and experience go into a <bold>grave</bold> where you fell instead of spilling on the ground. Go back and open it to get them back.

### Header: Lava and the void
Die in lava or fall into the void and there is no grave: what you carried is gone. In the End a void anchor can save you from the fall.
