The chapters' own patterns go on banners the way vanilla's do: in a <citem 'minecraft:loom'>, in any dye colour, Cerise, Laserviolet and RGB included. Craft the banner onto a shield and the shield shows it too.

### Header: In any loom
<bold>ITK</bold>, <bold>QMISK</bold> and <bold>TMEIT</bold> need no pattern item. Put a banner and a dye in any loom and pick them from the list.

### Header: Pirkko
The <bold>Pirkko</bold> pattern needs its pattern item, the <citem 'kultur:pirkko_banner_pattern'>, in the loom, like the flower or creeper pattern. The item is not used up. It can't be found in survival yet.

### Header: The painting
Hang a <citem 'minecraft:painting'> on a wall with room for one 3 wide and 4 high and you may get IT's own: <bold>The Guardian of Kistan</bold>, by Emelie Stark.<nl>

<gray>All of these are IT's. Data and Media have none yet.</gray>
