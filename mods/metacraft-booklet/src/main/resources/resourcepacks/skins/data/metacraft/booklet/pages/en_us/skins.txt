### Header: Getting one
A skin comes as a <bold>Skin Token</bold>. Right-click the token and confirm to claim it: the skin is yours and the token is used up.

Tokens come out of <bold>Skin Boxes</bold>. Hold a box in your main hand and right-click to see what it holds and the chances; press Roll to open it and get one token. You can trade both boxes and tokens.

<bold>Mob hats.</bold> Now and then a zombie, skeleton or creeper spawns wearing a hat. The hat itself doesn't drop, but the mob drops a <bold>Classic Hats Box</bold>.

### Header: /itemskins
<yellow>/itemskins</yellow> opens the Skin Menu: every skin on the server, yours in colour and the rest as grey "???". Put a skin on any item of the right kind you carry, a sword, a pickaxe, a helmet and so on. A skin is on one item at a time.

A skin only shows while the item is with you. Lend it and it looks plain.

Don't want a skin any more? <bold>Extract</bold> it in the Skin Menu and it turns back into a token, to trade or give away.

### Header: Hats
Many hats can be dyed like leather armour, in the crafting grid. A cauldron washes the dye off.

<gray>The Skin Menu has a "How skins work" book with the rest.</gray>
